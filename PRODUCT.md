# Tether: product context

Written from the team's brief, not from an interview. Lines marked *assumed* are inferences to confirm.

- **Users.** Astronauts on long missions assessing their own health, with no medical training assumed. *Assumed:* used once a day on a tablet or phone-sized display in dim cabin light.
- **Job.** Log a handful of readings in about a minute, see at a glance whether anything has moved, and know what to do and who to tell.
- **Surface mode.** Operate. The interface should disappear into the task.
- **Look.** Dark, simple, and legible in the spirit of consumer fitness bands: one score ring, large condensed numerals, three status colours. Must not look machine-made.
- **Brand.** Tether: the line that keeps a crew member connected to their own baseline and to the ground. States are Steady, Drifting, and Off baseline.
- **Accessibility.** Status is always colour plus shape plus word. Body text contrast at least 4.5:1. Touch targets at least 44 px. Full keyboard use. Reduced motion respected.
- **Voice.** Plain, direct, second person. Tell the reader what to do, then why.
